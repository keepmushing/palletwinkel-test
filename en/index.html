Export

Export crates carrying our own ISPM-15 mark.

Wooden packaging leaving Europe must be phytosanitary treated and marked. We buy treated timber, saw and build in house, and apply the mark under our own registration BE-1689.

That means the crate you order is marked here — not elsewhere, and not after the fact.
