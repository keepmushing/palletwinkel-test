    Where it is made

    Our own workshop in Wingene, Belgium.

    We saw, assemble and mark everything ourselves. No middlemen and no subcontracting chain: what you order is built here, by the people you speak to.

    What we make

    Not a catalogue. The right solution for your load.

    Six families of made-to-measure woodwork, plus export boxes with our own ISPM-15 mark. Each one is built for the weight, the shape and the journey it has to survive.

    Custom pallets

    Block and stringer pallets in any size, calculated for your weight, stacking method and lifting direction.

    Crates and boxes

    Closed cases and open crates, on a timber base frame or on a full pallet floor.

    Glass racks

    Glass racks and plywood trays built to your drawing, for glass that must not shift or break.

    Pallet collars

    Collars and lids that turn an existing pallet into a closed, stackable unit.

    Boards and panels

    Timber and sheet material cut and trimmed to size, ready for collection or delivery.

    Wooden constructions

    Skids, transport cradles and support frames for loads that do not fit an ordinary pallet.

    Export

    Export boxes carrying our own ISPM-15 mark.

    Wooden packaging leaving Europe must be phytosanitary treated and marked. We buy treated timber, saw and build in house, and apply the mark under our own registration BE-1689.

    That means the box you order is marked here — not elsewhere, and not after the fact.
